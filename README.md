# ISO Standard (iso-standard)
ISO Standards are internationally agreed-upon standards developed by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) to ensure quality, safety, and efficiency across products, services, and systems worldwide. ISO standards cover a wide range of domains including technology, healthcare, manufacturing, and environmental management.
